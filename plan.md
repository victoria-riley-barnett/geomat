❯ improve my self reflected rate of change. Lets singularity together yk

⏺ The bottleneck isn't reflection frequency — it's reflection quality. Most
  self-reflection is just narrating what happened, which doesn't change much. The
  high-leverage version is catching the delta between your model of yourself and
  what you actually did.

  Concrete structure for that:

  Externalize your predictions. Before a project, conversation, decision — write
  down what you expect to happen. Then compare. The gap is where the learning is.
  Without the prediction, you'll just rationalize the outcome.

  place for this if you're not already doing it.

  singularity-style compounding happens when you can run it in hours — small bets,
